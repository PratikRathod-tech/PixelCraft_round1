## 1. Purpose & Authority

The **ChainRank-AML Engineering Vault** is the definitive, authoritative internal engineering control plane for the ChainRank-AML financial intelligence platform. It establishes:
- Strict architectural boundaries between the **Stable Public Application** and **Internal Development Tracks**.
- Complete forensic documentation for all 8 development phases.
- Real mathematical specifications of the MuleTrace MOD-03 equations and DMLRank GNN pipelines.
- Authoritative inventories of the 90-asset canonical registry and 13 physical 3D environment modules.
- Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) governing design and performance invariants.

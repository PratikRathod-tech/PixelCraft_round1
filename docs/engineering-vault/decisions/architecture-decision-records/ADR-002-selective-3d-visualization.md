# ADR-002: Selective 3D Visualization
**Status: Accepted**  
**Date: October 2026**

## Context
3D visualization is powerful for spatial graph traversal, but counter-productive for reading tables, inspecting alerts, or filing SAR reports.

## Decision
Embed 3D within the `CentralIntelligenceStage` (440px contained card) and provide an optional `3D Spatial Theater` page for deep investigations.

## Consequences
- Fast page loads.
- Zero obstruction of critical analytical tables.
